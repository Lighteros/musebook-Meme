Your path, one step at a time

New here? Start with 1. Already set up? Skip ahead.

  1. 1

    Get set up

    Use a wallet compatible with Robinhood Chain, such as Robinhood Wallet or MetaMask. Keep a little ETH for network fees (“gas”).

    New wallet? Save its recovery phrase privately. On a phone, open the bridge and trading links in your wallet’s browser if connecting is difficult.

    Set up your wallet & network ↗
  2. 2

    Bridge to Robinhood Chain

    Get ETH in your wallet, then use a bridge to move it to Robinhood Chain. A bridge moves funds between networks.

    Choose your starting network and Robinhood Chain as the destination. Review the fee, confirm in your wallet, and wait for the ETH to arrive. Keep some ETH for network fees (“gas”).

    Choose a bridge in Robinhood’s guide ↗
  3. 3

    Swap into $MUSEBOOK

    Use a swap service that supports Robinhood Chain. Select Robinhood Chain and find $musebook using the full token address above. Only continue if that exact token is available and the service can provide a quote.

    Enter the amount to spend. Check the token address, fees, price impact and minimum tokens you’ll receive. Approve only when the quote looks right to you.

A few helpful signposts

I don’t have any crypto yet

Buy ETH through a provider available in your country, then send it to your own wallet on a network the bridge supports. Match the withdrawal network to the bridge’s starting network. Check the provider’s minimum withdrawal and fees before buying.

My ETH is on another network

ETH on Ethereum or Base is not yet ETH on Robinhood Chain. Follow step 2. Your wallet must have ETH on the starting network to cover any fees there, too.

The trade won’t go through

Check that your wallet is on Robinhood Chain and has ETH left for gas. If your swap service cannot find a route, or the price impact is too high, stop and try again later. Don’t keep raising slippage to force the trade.

I bought it, but can’t see my tokens

First check that your trade confirmed in your wallet’s activity. Switch to Robinhood Chain; if needed, use your wallet’s import-token option with the full token contract above. Copying an address here does not add a token to your wallet automatically.

A little care goes a long way.

Start small. Tokens can lose value, and network fees still apply. Never share your recovery phrase.

Swaps happen through your chosen trading service, with each approval in your wallet. The token contract above identifies $musebook; don’t send funds to it.
